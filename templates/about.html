About QRGEN

A simple and smart solution for QR code generation and scanning.

Who We Are

QRGEN is a simple and smart web-based application developed to make QR code generation and scanning easier and more convenient.

Our platform allows users to convert text and website URLs into QR codes and scan existing QR code images to retrieve their stored information.

Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify digital information sharing through an easy-to-use QR code platform. We aim to provide users with a convenient way to generate, download and scan QR codes.

Our Vision

Our vision is to make digital information sharing faster and more accessible by providing simple, reliable and user-friendly QR code solutions.

Technologies Used

QRGEN is developed using modern web technologies and Python-based tools.

Python

Handles the backend logic and QR code operations.

Flask

Connects the frontend pages with Python functionality.

HTML & CSS

Creates the structure and visual design of the website.

JavaScript

Supports interactive elements on the website.

OpenCV

Detects and decodes QR codes from uploaded images.
